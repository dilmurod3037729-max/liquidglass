# Third-party notices

The libraries in `web/`, `win-native/` and `python/` have no runtime dependencies beyond the platform APIs and PySide6 (LGPL-3.0, installed by the user).

The optional native demo in `core/native` downloads these at build time (they are not distributed in this repository):

- [GLFW](https://www.glfw.org/) (zlib licence)
- [Dear ImGui](https://github.com/ocornut/imgui) (MIT)
